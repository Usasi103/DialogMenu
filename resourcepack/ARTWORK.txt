DialogMenu original stone UI (2026-10-03)

Settings controls, 16x16 settings icons, dialogue/template panels and quest controls
were authored in Blockbench for DialogMenu. Editable sources and provenance are
included in the source repository under design/original-ui.

The settings and template artwork no longer uses the former third-party server
sprite sources. Legacy skin names and glyph allocations remain compatible.

Minecraft font/item references and Unifont keep their own attribution. See
assets/dialogmenu_settings/font/LICENSE-Unifont.txt and the repository LICENSE.
